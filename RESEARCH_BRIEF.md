## Primary insight

**Clock-Incommensurability Class (CIC).** After the subtractions above, the remaining unknown is that **observation cadence cannot resolve process cadence**, so every named scientific "object" is a freeze-frame of a process we have not watched.

Three operators inside CIC, plus one iteration split:

1. **Cadence mismatch** — same object; process timescale τ_p ≫ observation window τ_o.
2. **Phase-object collapse (POC)** — a freeze-frame is promoted to an ontological class (a type of thing) instead of a phase of a process.
3. **Loss-outruns-catalog (LOC)** — the naming clock is slower than the loss/extinction clock. The dark majority is not waiting to be found. It is disappearing unnamed.
4. **Two-clock metrology** (iteration, after the first engine inflated evolutionary age as τ_p) — two legitimate measurement systems close the same question differently. Distinct from NCR: NCR is "one named cause does not add up." Two-clock is "two named clocks do not agree on what the question is."

**Falsification rule.**

- If a time-series of the *same* object/process closes the gap → CIC for that case.
- If more spatial samples at the same cadence close it → undersampling (already claimed).
- If locating or removing a converter closes it → TAC (already claimed).
- If adding more of the named cause closes it → not NCR; CIC does not apply.
- If a discriminator exists and has been run → the split is settled; drop.

Scores are a classification heuristic over sourced gaps. They are **not measurements of nature**.

## What already exists (orientation, sourced)

### Space

- Dark matter remains unidentified; dark energy's origin unknown (NASA). Not reclaimed.
- Hubble tension: local H0 ~73 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ vs Planck/CMB ~67–68. JWST confirmed the local ladder is not a crowding error (Riess / SH0ES; Keck time-delay 5 Dec 2025). **RETAIN. Spoken. TAC item 10 / NCR retain.**
- Little red dots (LRDs): compact, red, point-like JWST sources at ~0.6–1.6 Gyr after the Big Bang; >300 observed as of 2025. Identity still debated (AGN vs compact starburst vs mixed). **New this window (not in the 30 Aug archive as a phase claim):**
  - Naidu et al., MIT, 12 Aug 2026: MoM-BH*-1 interpreted as a **black hole star** — a ~10⁵ M☉ black hole inside a solar-system-scale hydrogen cocoon. Observation confirmed; class interpretation **unproved**.
  - ScienceDaily / NASA, 17 Aug 2026: LRDs may not be a separate galaxy population (hidden-host / Saguaro case).
  - MIT Physics, 1 Sep 2026: Naidu discusses LRDs as black hole stars (The John Batchelor Show).
  - arXiv 2608.29011, 2608.28975 (1 Sep 2026): follow-up LRD dust and neutrino papers listing Naidu.

### Earth

- Direct sample: Kola Superdeep ~12.3 km vs radius 6371 km ≈ **0.19% of the way to the core**. Undersampling / NCR. Zeroed as control.
- Deep biosphere: ~15% of Earth's biomass; ~90% of archaeal/bacterial biomass (Bar-On 2018). Generation times tens to thousands of years (Jørgensen 2011, 2016); biomass turnover up to hundreds of thousands of years. Existence already claimed as Dark Biosphere Hypothesis. The **clock** is the CIC remainder, heavily penalized for prior overlap.
- Crustal "dark oxygen" in 1.2-Gyr brine, Moab Khotsong: 0.09 µmol L⁻¹ O₂; mechanism biology vs radiolysis **unproved** (Ruff; New Yorker, Dinneen, 13 Aug 2026; NASA OxyMoRon). TAC owns the converter. Not reclaimed.
- **Murali et al., Science** (DOI 10.1126/science.adp1853): some aerobes exist in genomes *before* oxygenic photosynthesis. Textbook sequence (photosynthesis → O₂ → aerobes / GOE ~2.4 Gya) is a named freeze-frame. Ancestral energy source **unproved**. Do not load-bear on Sweetman nodules.

### Ocean

- Seafloor mapped to modern standards: **28.7% as of 20 Apr 2026** (Seabed 2030 / NOAA Ocean Exploration, updated 20 Apr 2026). Undersampling. Control.
- Deep seafloor visually observed: **<0.001%** (NOAA citing *Sci. Adv.* 10.1126/sciadv.adp8602). Deep ocean >90% of ocean volume. Undersampling. Control.
- Marine animal species: 700,000–1,000,000 estimated; roughly two-thirds undescribed (NOAA / Mora et al. *Current Biology*).
- Nodule dark oxygen (Sweetman et al., *Nature Geoscience* 17, 737–744, 2024): **contested**. Editor's Note 8 Apr 2026; Downes et al. *Front. Mar. Sci.* 2025 (10.3389/fmars.2025.1721853); Live Science 19 Mar 2026 thermodynamics critique; DORI landers 2026 pending. Homonym collapse still active vs crustal O₂. **RETAIN. Do not load-bear.**

### Animals

- Insects: ~5.5 million estimated, ~1 million named, **>80% undescribed** (Lehmitz et al., *npj Biodivers.* 2025, s44185-025-00108-3). ~10,000 insect species described per year → **~450 years** to finish. Twenty families (dark taxa, half Diptera) = >50% of local flying-insect richness, and they are the most taxonomically neglected.
- Li et al. 2025 (PMC12680044): at current rates it will take **>400 years** to describe even a small fraction of insect diversity.
- Stork / Griffith, 26 Aug 2024: undescribed insects are **smaller, rarer, more range-restricted, and more extinction-prone** than named ones. In one Borneo rove-beetle sample, 76% of 252 species were new to science.
- Each morphological insect species conceals, on average, **~3.1 cryptic species** (Lehmitz 2025).
- Magnetoreceptor cell: unlocated after ~60 years. **RETAIN (TAC).**

### Humans

- ~98% of the genome is non-coding ("dark genome"). Function still unsettled. ENCODE-style assay clocks vs Graur-style mutational/selection clocks still disagree (IISc Connect, Apr 2026). NCR listed the naming drift junk → dark → regulatory. CIC remainder is specifically **two-clock disagreement about what "function" means**.
- Consciousness discriminator, anesthesia maintenance: spoken / NCR. Zeroed.

### Land

- Omnitrophota: globally common, almost uncultured. **RETAIN (TAC).**
- Functions of most soil-relevant prokaryotic species unknown (Lehmitz 2025). 87–99% of microbial diversity uncultured across eight habitats (analysis of 1,046 studies, preprint 3 Jul 2025). Cultivation still <1% in many ecosystems (Alanzi 2025).

## Discriminators (runnable, not decorative)

**Insects (LOC).** Compare description rate vs independently estimated extinction/range-loss for the same dark-taxa families (the Diptera-heavy twenty). If unnamed species disappear faster than they are named in a protected-area time series, LOC is confirmed. If speeding up DNA barcoding *without* reducing loss closes the unnamed fraction, this was undersampling, not LOC.

**Little red dots (POC).** Do not wait 300 Myr. Use a redshift baseline of the *same* selection function. If the LRD fraction collapses into ordinary hosts plus a short-lived black-hole-star phase across z, POC is confirmed. If LRDs remain a disjoint class at every z they appear, POC is wrong. Naidu 12 Aug 2026 and the Saguaro host-hiding study are the live tests.

**Cryptic species (POC+LOC).** For a closed morphological set, add nuclear + mitochondrial delimitation. If the cryptic factor holds near 3.1 and the unnamed splits are the range-restricted ones, the freeze-frame was never the object.

**Aerobes-before-photosynthesis (two-clock).** Independent phylogenomic clocks (not only the Murali tree) must still place aerobic respiration before oxygenic photosynthesis. The ancestral *energy source* of those aerobes is a separate unproved claim.

**Dark genome (two-clock vs NCR).** Ask one question two ways: reproducible biochemical activity vs purifying-selection signatures. If they remain irreconcilable, two-clock stands. If one operational definition of "function" makes them agree, this was NCR naming, not dual metrology.

**Soil function.** Resample the same plot across seasons and years with matched metagenome+metabolome. Seasonal phase of known taxa → cadence/POC. New uncultured lineages faster than functions assigned → LOC.
